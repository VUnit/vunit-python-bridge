The bridge supports a 64-bit `integer`, as NVC has with VHDL-2019. Integer results, `integer_vector`, `integer_vector_ptr_t` and `integer_array_t` values and `dict_t` entries used to be garbage with it. Integers now have the full range of `integer`, except the integers of a `dict_t` and the elements of an `integer_array_t`, which VUnit stores in 32 bits.
