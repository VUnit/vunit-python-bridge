With a 64-bit `integer`, as with VHDL-2019, integer values crossing the bridge were garbage. They are now transferred correctly. `integer`, `integer_vector` and `integer_vector_ptr_t` values have the full 64-bit range, while the integers of a `dict_t` and the elements of an `integer_array_t` remain 32 bits. A larger value is reported as an error, except in a `dict_t` argument, where VUnit's `set_integer` has already truncated it.
